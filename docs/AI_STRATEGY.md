# Tohbala OS AI Strategy

## Overview

The Tohbala OS AI strategy is focused on transforming business information into actionable intelligence.

The goal is not to create another general-purpose chatbot.

The goal is to build an AI business intelligence layer that understands:

- Companies
- Customer problems
- Business opportunities
- Technology needs
- Sales activity
- Market signals

and helps organizations make better decisions.

---

# AI Vision

The future of business operations will require systems that can:

- Understand business context
- Identify opportunities
- Recommend actions
- Predict outcomes
- Automate repetitive workflows

Tohbala OS is designed to become an intelligent business operating system that assists organizations throughout their growth journey.

---

# AI Opportunity Scanner

The first AI capability planned for Tohbala OS is the **AI Opportunity Scanner**.

The Opportunity Scanner will analyze business information and identify potential opportunities.

It will evaluate:

- Company information
- Industry trends
- Business challenges
- Technology requirements
- Existing opportunities
- Customer interactions

and generate intelligence.

# Responsible AI Principles

Tohbala OS AI development will focus on:

## Human Decision Support

AI provides recommendations.

Humans make final decisions.

---

## Data Privacy

Business information must be protected through:

- Secure authentication
- Permission controls
- Data protection practices

---

## Transparency

AI recommendations should provide understandable reasoning.

Users should understand:

- What was analyzed
- Why a recommendation was made
- What actions are suggested
