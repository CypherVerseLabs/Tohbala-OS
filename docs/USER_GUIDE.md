# Tohbala OS User Guide

## Overview

Welcome to Tohbala OS.

Tohbala OS is a business operating system designed to help organizations manage relationships, discover opportunities, track sales pipelines, and prepare for AI-powered business intelligence.

The platform brings together:

- Company management
- Opportunity tracking
- Pipeline management
- Activity history
- Business analytics
- Future AI recommendations

---

# Company Management

## Purpose

The Company module stores and manages business relationships.

Users can:

- Create companies
- View company information
- Update company details
- Track related opportunities
- Review relationship history

---

## Creating a Company

Steps:

1. Navigate to Companies
2. Select "New Company"
3. Enter company information
4. Save the record

Company information may include:

- Company name
- Industry
- Website
- Size
- Location
- Contact details
- Business description

---

# Opportunity Management

## Purpose

Opportunities represent potential business engagements.

Each opportunity captures:

- Customer challenge
- Proposed solution
- Technology requirements
- Estimated value
- Sales progress
- Follow-up actions

---

## Creating an Opportunity

Steps:

1. Open Opportunities
2. Select "New Opportunity"
3. Select or create a company
4. Enter business information
5. Assign pipeline stage
6. Save opportunity
